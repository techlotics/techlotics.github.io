Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy explains how Techlotics (Philippines) and its automation tool "Techlotics Robot" handle data. Techlotics Robot is a private tool used only by the Techlotics owner to help make product videos. It is not offered to the public.

What Techlotics Robot accesses

  • Google Drive: with the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the robot lists, downloads, moves and uploads files in the owner's own Google Drive, in the folders made for video editing (for example "Techlotics Auto Edit"). It works with the photos and video clips placed there, and the finished videos it creates.
  • Public YouTube data: a separate daily trend report reads public video information (titles, descriptions, view counts) through the YouTube Data API. It does not access any personal YouTube account.

How the data is used

  • Photos and clips are used only to edit them into a video for the owner.
  • Editing happens on Techlotics' own private computer at home, not on third-party servers. A small local AI model may look at a few frames to name the product shown; it also runs on that same computer.
  • Temporary copies made while editing are deleted after each video is finished.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to train general AI models.
  • We do not access files outside the editing folders, or anyone else's Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Techlotics Robot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping it safe

Access keys are stored only inside the automation software on Techlotics' private computer, which is not open to the internet.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Techlotics Robot → Remove access.
